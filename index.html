Founder Story

Built by a creator, for creators

Nur AI wasn't designed in a boardroom. It was reverse-engineered from what actually worked.

Bilal Mohammed — Bilal .Bax — built Baxx Thobes the hard way first: filming his own footage, learning the grade, testing hooks, teaching himself which nasheed landed on which cut, and reaching out to brands with no idea what to charge.

What came out of that was a repeatable chain: research the angle, review the video, then build the plan — same order, every time. Bilal refined those workflows while growing Baxx Thobes to 35,000 followers and learning what consistently improves short-form content — without relying on haram visuals or trending pop songs.

Nur AI is that studio, offered to other creators — with Maryam, Aisha and Ahmad reviewing your content the way he learned to review his own.

"I'm not selling you content. I'm giving you the exact team I had to be."
— Bilal .Bax, Co-founder

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the video audit really free?
Yes. Send one video and you'll receive a full audit within 24–48 hours — no credit card, no catch.
What kind of videos can I submit?
An Instagram Reel, a TikTok, a YouTube Short, a public video URL, or a shareable Google Drive / Dropbox link.
Do you edit or publish the video for me?
No. Nur AI is a content strategy and video-review studio — you get the plan, the feedback and the direction, and you keep full creative control of your work.
Is the guidance really 100% halal?
Yes. Everything is built halal-first — vocal-only nasheeds, no instrumentation, nothing haram. Maryam sets the standard and Ahmad checks it.
Do I need a drone or a gimbal?
No. Recommendations default to the DJI Osmo Pocket 3, but tell us what you shoot on — phone included — and the advice adapts to your kit.
Can Nur AI help me with CapCut?
Yes. Maryam can guide you from beginner level — fonts, grading, clean visuals and the edits behind the Nur AI look.
What happens after the free audit?
If you'd like ongoing help, the studio offers deeper strategy, research and review support. There's no obligation — just reply to your audit and we'll take it from there.
Will more services be added?
Yes — new capabilities are being added continuously as the studio grows toward a full creative platform.

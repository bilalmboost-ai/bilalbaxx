Camera Baseline

Settings, not guesses

Nur AI ships exact settings with every shot list. Default baseline is the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 — fixed f/2.0, so exposure is shutter, ISO and ND only. Shooting on something else? Tell Maryam and she adapts the whole list.

  • 4K/60 default, slowed to 40% in CapCut. 4K/120 reserved for peak moments.
  • D-Log M 10-bit always — the Normal profile bakes in contrast that kills the grade.
  • 180° shutter: 1/120 at 60fps, 1/240 at 120fps, 1/50 at 24fps.
  • Manual everything — WB, ISO and shutter locked per scene, re-locked when the light changes.
  • Gimbal: Follow for tracking, Tilt Locked for low glides, FPV for whip pans, ActiveTrack solo.
  • As light drops: remove ND → drop frame rate → raise ISO, capped at 1600.
